Privacy, in plain language

Your dossier belongs to you. It is encrypted in your browser and is not uploaded to us.

What stays on your device

Record locations, contacts, instructions, review history, and profile details are encrypted using Web Crypto before being stored in IndexedDB on your device. Your passphrase is held only in memory while the dossier is open. It is not stored or transmitted. We cannot decrypt or recover your dossier.

What we collect

This version has no analytics, advertising, accounts, cookies, or telemetry.

Exports and deletion

Encrypted JSON backups remain protected by the passphrase used when exported. CSV exports and printed pages are readable, so you control their physical and digital security. Settings → Delete this dossier removes the encrypted local copy.

Offline cache

The service worker caches application resources, including the illustration, so the app opens without a connection. Your dossier records are not placed in the cache.
